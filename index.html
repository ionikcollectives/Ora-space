A walawwa, opened again.

ORA is a heritage house among old trees, opened again for the slow things. Coffee that is never rushed. Art on walls that remember. Music on the lawn, films under the branches, and books read slowly. A place to feel, and to belong.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What people ask us about ORA Space, the café, art space and creative house opening in a heritage walawwa in Sri Lanka.

What is ORA Space?

ORA Space is a café, art space and creative house in a walawwa, a Sri Lankan heritage house, among old trees. Under one roof you will find an unhurried café, old walls for exhibitions, a quiet library, a studio for sound and image, film evenings under the trees, music and theatre on the lawn, and room for private events.

When does ORA Space open, and what are the hours?

ORA opens soon. Join the Opening List and we will send you the date and the first programme before anyone else. Once open, the house is open from 8.00 am to 10.00 pm.

Where is ORA Space?

In a heritage walawwa among old trees in Sri Lanka. The full address goes to the Opening List first and is shared here at launch.

Can I exhibit my art at ORA Space?

Yes. We are opening the walls of the house to new paintings, prints and photographs, and choosing our first exhibitions now. Join the Opening List, tick Art and tell us about your work.

Can I perform, or bring a film night to ORA?

Acoustic sets, poetry, theatre and film evenings happen on the lawn and under the trees, for small audiences in the open air. Join the Opening List and tick Music & Stage or Film.

Can I book ORA Space for a private event or a shoot?

Yes. Shoots, gatherings and private evenings can be held in the house and gardens. Tell us what you are planning and we will shape the space around it.

Is there a studio I can use?

Yes. The studio is for sound, image and ideas: record, shoot, rehearse or simply think. Our first studio members get founding rates, so join the Opening List and tick Studio.
